## Why one endpoint feeds 33 and 34 at once (strong evidence)

34's breadth, movers and index options are not separate data. They are the screener's counts and rows. The example
generator checks this on the 12 Oct data and fails if any one differs:
- every breadth count (all, n500, US ours) equals the counts over 33's rows;
- all 8 Nifty 500 gainers and all 8 "All NSE" most-traded equal 33's rows sorted by d1 or value;
- the four index-option rows equal 33's F&O rows field for field.

Serving them twice would publish one fact twice. These endpoints carry `x-shared`, and 34 reads them with query
parameters (`u=n500&sort=d1&page_size=8`; `symbols=NIFTY,BANKNIFTY,FINNIFTY,MIDCPNIFTY`).

## Questions for the owner (the contract is ambiguous here; nothing was changed)

1. **Who publishes NSE's daily files?** exepex/trading (DESK_DECISIONS, 12 Oct: "F&O screener rows need a new
   publishing story") or a shared `platform/` ingest. Both notes.md leave it open. The contract names the publisher

2. **34's flows "period total"**: the data has 5 days of FII/DII, enough for 1D and 1W only. Should the stock tool

   `major_event_names`), which gives none on 12 Oct.
