# 39 Today's card: why it looks like this

- **The verdict first, in three channels.** TRADE is green with a tick, NO TRADE red with a block sign, WATCH teal
  with a flask; each says its word (owner's rule: never colour alone). The reason is one plain sentence under it.
- **The three times in their order.** Market data frozen, decision final, card sent: the order is the honesty of the
  card (nothing after 15:00 enters it), so it is shown, not hidden in a footer.
- **A picture for each number.** The entry range is a bar with the "do not enter above" line; the maximum loss is a bar
  against the Rs 10,000 cap; the forecast is a chance bar; the strategy's progress is a five-rung ladder.
- **Its record next to every chance.** "It said 30-40% on 140 days; it happened on 41%" tells a beginner how far to
  trust the number; a forecaster with no record says so.
- **Real money: not eligible, always visible.** The contract cannot say otherwise until the real-money gate exists.
- **Owner only is marked where it applies** (a lock on the position): the live strikes never enter a public repository.
- **States are Mock and say so** (the Mock tag, the made-up future date); the default is the empty case, because that
  is the real shape today.
