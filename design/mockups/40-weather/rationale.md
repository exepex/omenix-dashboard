# 40 Market weather: why it looks like this

- **One answer first:** the colour as a big verdict (glyph, word, colour) and what it means today ("No new paper trade
  today" or "New paper trades are allowed").
- **The chance against the usual chance, as a picture.** Each bar has a black tick at the usual chance, so "2.8 times
  the usual" is seen, not computed.
- **Why, in plain words:** the drivers with Riskier or Calmer, so the card can say why in one line.
- **Its record, honestly:** the last sessions' colours next to what happened, how often it is RED against its 15%
  limit (with "over-alarming" when it is above), its miss score against the usual chance, and whether RED cost or saved
  money this month. A survival rule kept by choice must show its price.
- **No weather is a state, not an error:** the missing input is named with the story that brings it.
