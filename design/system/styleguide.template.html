Design system

Cockpit design system

One light scheme, v2 "modern admin light" (the look of modern admin templates such as Pixinvent's Materialize, applied to a broker-style cockpit): a soft light-grey page, white cards lifted by a faint shadow, one periwinkle-indigo accent (#5a5ee6), a dark navy sidebar with labels, icon tiles on KPI cards, gradient area charts, green #15803d / red #dc2626 for signals and signed numbers. System font stack, vendored Material Symbols. Everything on this page is rendered from tokens.css + components.css + icons.svg; contrast ratios are computed in the page.

Verdict header

The decision page's header. NO is red. YES is green: a darker green for a strong signal (forecaster confidence ≥ 0.75 or anchored probability ≥ 0.65), a lighter green for an okay one. The numbers below are the real HDFC Bank values of 6 Oct 2026 for NO; the two YES states show the component only (no live YES call exists yet, so their text is the rule, not data).

Why: the model gives a rise only 47% (1 day) and 47% (5 days) — a coin flip — and in the back-test it has shown no skill; the forecaster abstained.

YESokay signal

Component state: a live up call with confidence below 0.75 and anchored probability below 0.65 (lighter green).

YESstrong signal

Component state: a live up call with confidence ≥ 0.75 or anchored probability ≥ 0.65 (darker green).

Colour roles

Each swatch shows the role, its hex and the WCAG contrast of its "on" colour (or of the role on the surface, for accents). Text on a container needs 4.5:1; large text and icons 3:1.

Signals: up / down / neutral / unclear, hit / miss

Never colour alone: an arrow, tick, cross or the sign plus the word. up #15803d vs down #dc2626 pass every dataviz checker gate (worst CVD ΔE 8.6 deutan).

Chart colours (dataviz skill, validated)

Series 1 is the accent. Status up/down against it pass all-pairs. Series 2-4 are reserved slots (validate with the dataviz checker before a chart uses more than one series).

Light fill = 80% band, dark fill = 50% band (series 1 at two alphas), ink line = actual close, hit/miss dot with glyph, published range as an outlined box with its number.
