# F&O data catalog (story P1, generated)

Generated by `python3 tools/api/render_fno_catalog.py` from `api/schemas/fno-*.yaml`, `api/paths/fno-screens.yaml`
and `api/examples/fno-screens.json`; do not edit by hand. The owner (15 Oct 2026): mock data shared by the front end
and the back end, so both can build their parts apart and integrate later. Every example is **Mock** (made-up numbers
on future dates); every money figure is **Paper**; the card says **PAPER ONLY**. Checked by
`python3 tools/api/check_fno_screens.py` here and by the pinned copy's tests in exepex/trading.

How to read the columns:
- **Visibility**: `public` may appear in the public repositories; `owner` is for the owner's signed-in dashboard only
  and never enters a public repository (live card legs and entry ranges: card records are kept in Google Drive only,
  owner 15 Oct 2026). Strategy internals are not a column: they are never sent (each screen lists them).
- **Backend today**: `yes` names the backend record that holds the value today (`<record>:<field>`, records below);
  `no` names the story that builds it. A `no` field is never filled with a made-up value: until its story is built, the
  page is not published (each endpoint answers 501). `provisional` marks a field whose shape waits for a protocol
  freeze (protocol v4, story G6, is not frozen yet): it may change when G6 freezes, and a change bumps the contract.
- **Source of truth (owner, 15 Oct 2026: "contract A"):** this repository's `api/` is the single master contract for
  stock and F&O; exepex/trading keeps a pinned byte copy with hashes and a drift check (`contracts/omenix-dashboard/pin.json`,
  `python -m fno market contract-check`).

## The daily trade card, PAPER ONLY: TRADE, NO TRADE with its reason, or WATCH

`GET /api/v1/markets/india/fno/card` · `getFnoCard` · fno.pages_history page card (P1 page; published by P2/P3 in the F&O publication)

- **Freshness:** fno_calendar; once a session day at card_sent_at (target 15:08 IST)
- **Empty state:** status NO TRADE, reason no_approved_strategy (the real state while no strategy has passed)
- **What it holds:** The day's trade card, PAPER ONLY. Empty state: status NO TRADE with reason no_approved_strategy (the real state while no strategy has passed); a missing input gives NO TRADE with reason data_check_failed and the input named
- **Mock examples:**
  - `india_trade` (positive): Mock (india): TRADE card, PAPER ONLY. A rule-based put spread approved to paper; AMBER weather; three objections, no hard-rule breach. Made-up numbers on a future date
  - `india_watch` (positive): Mock (india): WATCH card, a shadow never traded. A hybrid candidate's shadow decision; the advocate did not answer in time, so code checks only
  - `india_red` (negative): Mock (india): NO TRADE, weather RED. RED blocks new entries; the price of staying out is not measured yet
  - `india_missing` (negative): Mock (india): NO TRADE, a missing input named. The data checks failed; each missing input is named with the story that brings it
  - `india_none` (empty): Mock (india): NO TRADE, no strategy has passed. The shape of the real state while no strategy is approved to paper, as a TEST post of live delivery (like T1's first post of 15 Oct 2026); the house forecast has no live record yet
- **Never sent (strategy internals):** the strategy's entry conditions, filters and thresholds (why it chose this day beyond the forecast and weather shown); the AI team's and the devil's advocate's prompts and model settings; the strategy's parameters and any fitted value
- **Fields:** 108, of which the backend can fill 29 today
